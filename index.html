01 / About

Curiosity, chemistry
and applied research.

My work connects synthesis and characterization with functional applications in sensing, catalysis and advanced materials.

I hold an MSc in Chemistry from Sister Nivedita University, Kolkata, and a BSc in Chemistry from Rama Devi Women's University, Bhubaneswar. My research experience spans hydrogel-based sensing platforms, metal-containing nanomaterials, carbon quantum dots and electrochemical studies.

I enjoy combining experimental chemistry with data interpretation and computational approaches to understand material properties and explore their potential applications. I am particularly interested in interdisciplinary research at the interface of chemistry, materials science and bioengineering.
